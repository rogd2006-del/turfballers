## Part 1: Project PRD/Brief Template

This template is designed to be comprehensive enough for most projects, balancing detail with readability.

---

### **Project PRD / Brief: [Your Project Title Here]**

**1. Document Meta-Information**
*   **Project Title:** [Clear, concise name of the project]
*   **Version:** 1.0
*   **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
*   **Author(s):** [Your Name/Team]
*   **Status:** Draft / Review / Approved

**2. Executive Summary**
*   [A short, 2-3 sentence overview of the project, its main goal, and expected impact. Think elevator pitch.]

**3. Problem Statement / Opportunity**
*   **What problem are we solving?** [Describe the user pain point or business challenge. Use data if available.]
*   **Who experiences this problem?** [Specify the target user segment or internal team affected.]
*   **Why is it important to solve now?** [Explain the urgency, impact of not solving it, or strategic alignment.]

**4. Goals & Objectives (SMART)**
*   **Overall Project Goal:** [High-level vision of what success looks like.]
*   **Specific, Measurable Objectives:**
    *   [Objective 1: e.g., Increase user retention by X% within Y months.]
    *   [Objective 2: e.g., Reduce customer support tickets related to Z by A%.]
    *   [Objective 3: e.g., Improve conversion rate for feature B by C%.]

**5. Target Audience**
*   **Who is this feature/product for?** [Describe the primary users (e.g., demographics, roles, behaviors, needs).]
*   **What are their core needs that this project addresses?**

**6. Solution Overview & Key Features**
*   **What are we building?** [A high-level description of the solution and how it addresses the problem.]
*   **Key Features/Capabilities:**
    *   [Feature 1: Name & brief description (e.g., "Interactive Onboarding Checklist: Guides new users through initial setup steps.")]
    *   [Feature 2: Name & brief description (e.g., "In-app Tutorial Videos: Short, context-sensitive videos explaining core functionalities.")]
    *   [Feature 3: ...]
*   **(Optional) User Stories (Example format):**
    *   As a [type of user], I want to [action], so that [benefit].

**7. Scope (In & Out)**
*   **In-Scope:** [What *is* included in this phase/release? Be specific.]
    *   e.g., "Web application functionality only."
    *   e.g., "Support for English language only."
*   **Out-of-Scope:** [What *is not* included in this phase/release? This is crucial for managing expectations.]
    *   e.g., "Mobile app integration will be considered in a later phase."
    *   e.g., "Advanced analytics dashboards are out of scope for MVP."

**8. Success Metrics & KPIs**
*   **How will we measure success against our objectives?**
    *   [Metric 1: e.g., Onboarding completion rate (Target: >80%)]
    *   [Metric 2: e.g., 7-day active user retention (Target: +15% from baseline)]
    *   [Metric 3: e.g., Time to first value/action (Target: < 5 minutes)]

**9. Technical Considerations (High-Level)**
*   [Any critical architectural decisions, integrations, platforms, or known technical challenges.]

**10. Dependencies & Risks**

    *   e.g., "Technical complexity delaying release."

**11. High-Level Timeline / Phasing (Optional)**

    *   e.g., Phase 1 (MVP): Q3 2024

**12. Stakeholders**

## Part 2: Hypothetical Example PRD (Based on made-up context)

Let's imagine the context is: "Our SaaS product, 'TaskFlow,' helps teams manage projects. We're seeing a significant drop-off for new users in the first week, especially those who haven't yet created their first project or invited team members. We need to improve our onboarding process to guide them to these key 'aha!' moments faster."

---

### **Project PRD / Brief: TaskFlow Onboarding Experience Revamp**

**1. Document Meta-Information**
*   **Project Title:** TaskFlow Onboarding Experience Revamp
*   **Version:** 1.0
*   **Date:** 2024-07-26
*   **Author(s):** Product Team
*   **Status:** Draft

**2. Executive Summary**
This project aims to enhance the TaskFlow onboarding experience to improve new user activation and retention. By providing clearer guidance to key setup actions (creating first project, inviting team members), we expect to significantly reduce early user churn and increase engagement.

**3. Problem Statement / Opportunity**
*   **What problem are we solving?** New TaskFlow users often struggle to initiate their first project and invite team members, leading to confusion and high churn rates within the first 7 days post-signup. Data shows 45% of new sign-ups don't complete these critical first steps.
*   **Who experiences this problem?** Primarily new users (SMBs, individual contributors, small teams) who are evaluating or newly adopting TaskFlow for project management.
*   **Why is it important to solve now?** High churn directly impacts our growth targets and customer acquisition cost (CAC). Improving onboarding is a critical lever for improving LTV and conversion from trial to paid.

**4. Goals & Objectives (SMART)**
*   **Overall Project Goal:** Significantly improve new user activation and 7-day retention by guiding users to core "aha!" moments faster.
*   **Specific, Measurable Objectives:**
    *   Increase the percentage of new users who create their first project by 20% (from 55% to 75%) within the first 7 days.
    *   Increase the percentage of new users who invite at least one team member by 15% (from 40% to 55%) within the first 7 days.
    *   Reduce 7-day churn for new sign-ups by 10%.

**5. Target Audience**
*   **Who is this feature/product for?** New TaskFlow users who have just completed signup, ranging from individual users setting up personal projects to team leads onboarding their small to medium-sized teams.
*   **What are their core needs that this project addresses?** They need clear, intuitive guidance to get started quickly, understand TaskFlow's value, and integrate it into their workflow without friction.

**6. Solution Overview & Key Features**
We will implement an interactive, step-by-step onboarding flow designed to guide users through initial setup. This will include nudges and clear calls-to-action for creating a project and inviting teammates.
*   **Key Features/Capabilities:**
    *   **Interactive Onboarding Checklist:** A persistent, collapsible checklist that highlights essential setup steps (e.g., "Create Your First Project," "Invite Your Team").
    *   **Contextual In-App Guidance:** Tooltips and modals that appear when a user hovers over or lands on relevant sections, providing quick explanations and prompts.
    *   **Personalized Welcome Flow:** A series of initial screens after signup that allows users to quickly define their use case (e.g., "Individual," "Small Team," "Enterprise") to tailor subsequent guidance.
    *   **"Quick Start" Project Templates:** Pre-populated project templates (e.g., "Marketing Campaign," "Software Development Sprint") to lower the barrier to creating a first project.
*   **(Optional) User Stories:**
    *   As a new user, I want a clear list of steps to get started, so I don't feel overwhelmed or lost.
    *   As a team lead, I want to easily invite my team members during setup, so we can collaborate from day one.
    *   As a new user, I want pre-built project ideas, so I can quickly create my first project without starting from scratch.

**7. Scope (In & Out)**
*   **In-Scope:**
    *   Enhancements to the web application's onboarding flow from initial login to 7-day post-signup.
    *   Updates to existing UI elements to support new guidance.
    *   Implementation of new in-app messaging and checklist components.
    *   A/B testing framework for onboarding variations.
*   **Out-of-Scope:**
    *   Overhaul of the mobile app onboarding experience (will be a subsequent project).
    *   Email drip campaigns for onboarding (Marketing owns this, though integration points will be considered).
    *   Major changes to core project management functionalities.

**8. Success Metrics & KPIs**
*   Onboarding checklist completion rate (Target: >85%).
*   Percentage of new users who create a project within 7 days (Target: >75%).
*   Percentage of new users who invite 1+ team members within 7 days (Target: >55%).
*   7-day active user retention rate (Target: +10% from current baseline).
*   Number of support tickets related to "getting started" queries (Target: -15%).

**9. Technical Considerations (High-Level)**
*   New frontend components for checklist, tooltips, and welcome flow (React framework).
*   Backend service updates to track user progress through onboarding steps.
*   Integration with existing analytics platform for tracking KPIs.
*   Ensure backward compatibility with existing user data structures.

**10. Dependencies & Risks**
*   **Dependencies:**
    *   Design team availability for UI/UX mockups and user testing.
    *   Marketing team collaboration for messaging consistency.
    *   Analytics team support for data tracking setup and reporting.
*   **Risks:**
    *   Scope creep if too many features are added to the onboarding flow.
    *   Negative impact on existing user experience if changes are not carefully contained.
    *   Technical complexity in accurately tracking granular onboarding steps.
*   **Mitigation Strategies:** Strict adherence to "in-scope" features, thorough user testing with existing users, phased rollout with A/B testing.

**11. High-Level Timeline / Phasing**
*   **Phase 1 (MVP):** Interactive Checklist, Contextual Guidance (8-10 weeks development + testing) - Target Q4 2024
*   **Phase 2 (Enhancements):** Personalized Welcome Flow, A/B Test winning variations (4-6 weeks) - Target Q1 2025

**12. Stakeholders**
*   **Core Team:** Product Manager (Lead), Engineering Lead, Lead Designer, QA Lead.
*   **Key Cross-Functional Stakeholders:** Head of Customer Success, Head of Marketing, Sales Leadership, VP of Product.

**13. Open Questions & Assumptions**
*   **Open Questions:**
    *   What is the optimal number of steps for the onboarding checklist before it becomes overwhelming?
    *   Should the checklist be dismissible, or always present until completed?
    *   What existing user data can we leverage to personalize the experience further?
*   **Assumptions:**
    *   Users want guidance and respond positively to in-app prompts.
    *   The core value proposition of TaskFlow is clear once users complete the first project and invite a team.
    *   Our analytics infrastructure is robust enough to track new granular events.

**14. Appendices (Optional)**
*   Link to Competitive Onboarding Analysis
*   Link to Initial Wireframes & User Flows
*   Link to User Interview Summaries (regarding onboarding friction)
